Size and sheet

Shell depth sets how far the paper reaches past the face: it wraps over the edge and lies on the back as a border. Measure the shells you buy; 4.5 is the press design's nominal.

Photos

Click a magnet on the sheet to select it, then click a photo to put it there. Drag the big preview to move the photo; use the slider or the scroll wheel to zoom.

Templates and printing

Use the blank template or the SVG guide to design in another app (Canva, Inkscape, Photoshop): put the photo over the whole cut area and keep text inside the dotted line.

  • Print at 100 % (“Actual size”), never “Fit to page”. The bar at the foot of the sheet must measure 50 mm.
  • Use 90–120 g/m² matte inkjet paper. Photo paper and card are too thick to fold round the edge.
  • Let inkjet prints dry for 10 minutes before pressing.
  • Cut on the grey line: a circle punch for round sizes; for the others a trimmer, then snip the four corners off along the short diagonal lines.
  • The shaded band on the big preview wraps round the edge; beyond the green line it lies on the back. Keep faces and front text inside the dotted line.
  • Text set to a “Back” position is printed in that border and reads upright on the back of the finished magnet. Check it with “Back, after pressing”.
