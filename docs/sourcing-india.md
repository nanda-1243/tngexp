# Building the MagPress M4 in India: where to get each part, and what it costs

Researched 28 Oct 2026 from suppliers' own pages, marketplace listings (IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Moglix) and institute rate
cards. Prices are what sellers advertise, mostly before GST (18 %) and transport. Treat every figure as ±30 % and get
two or three written quotes against the drawings before ordering. Andhra Pradesh and Delhi NCR come first in every row;
the "best option" column names the place that is best on price, capability or delivery wherever it is.

For one or two sizes, a bought press is cheaper. The MagPress pays off only if you want several sizes on one frame, the
heavier build, or the printed back border it is designed around.

Ways to bring it down: order the frame from a private CNC shop and only the kit from a tool room; buy induction-hardened
chrome bar for the columns and hardened ground dowel stock for the pins; skip QPQ, Nord-Lock and powder coat on the first
build.

## 7. How to order

1. Send the drawing PDF and the DXF zip from `release/RevE/` (and the STEP files from `models/step/` if the shop has CAM),
   with the material and heat-treatment column of the parts list. Ask for a quote per part, and for the inspection
   report on the H7 bores, the 210.00 hole pitch (base, crown, ram) and the kit openings.
2. Kit parts: rough machine, vacuum harden, then wire-EDM and grind, in that order. Ask the tool room to arrange the
   hardening if it has no vacuum furnace.
3. Buy the springs, bushings, pins and fasteners yourself before the parts arrive, so the shop can test-fit them.
4. Start with one size kit. Order the next kits after the first magnets come out right.
