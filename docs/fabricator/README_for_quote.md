# Photo-magnet press: drawings for a cost estimate

For: Vaishnavi Engineering Industries. Date: 29 October 2026.

There are two press designs. Please quote each one separately. Both are hand-lever presses that make fridge magnets from
tin, photo, film, an acrylic card and a magnet sheet.

## 1. LIGHT: MagPress L1 (revision L1-A), first choice

One die set for 63.5 × 63.5 mm square magnets. About 26 kg in total.

## Notes for the quote

- **No sheet-metal bending.** Every part is a flat profile cut from plate (laser, plasma or waterjet; wire-EDM for the
  hardened die openings) and then machined, or a turned part (columns, pins). So the DXF files are the flat cutting
  profiles. No unfolded patterns are needed.
- **Please split the price into:**
  - material;
  - profile cutting;
  - machining;
  - wire-EDM;
  - heat treatment;
  - grinding;
  - finishing (powder coat, black oxide, anodise);
  - bought parts (springs, screws, bushes: the BOM lists them);
  - assembly.

  Please also give it separately for the frame and the die set, and give the lead time.
- **Light die set: two options, please price both.**
  - Hardened D2 (58–60 HRC, wire-EDM after hardening).
  - A first prototype in P20 / 1.2312 pre-hardened, with no heat treatment.
- **Light die parts may change slightly.** The material sizes are assumed until the owner measures the material
  supplier's pre-cut set (drawing L-003). The die parts marked MAT (pedestal, ring A, cup ring, riser, upper ring,
  plug) may change by a few tenths of a mm before manufacture. Quote them as drawn; final drawings will follow
  before work starts.
- **Tolerances:** ISO 2768-mK unless a drawing says otherwise. Fits and critical sizes are on the drawings and in the
  feature tables. The PDF drawings govern over the DXF and STEP files.
